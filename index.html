About Us

Shree Sahjanand Dabeli is a Gujarati food stall located at Chowpatty, right in front of Punjab National Bank. Started by Mr. Rakesh Bhanushali and his family, we bring you the authentic taste of Gujarat through our delicious and freshly prepared Dabeli. With traditional flavors, quality ingredients, and a passion for serving great food, we aim to give every customer a true taste of Gujarat—authentic, flavorful, and made with love.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dabeli?

Dabeli is a popular Gujarati street food made with a spiced potato filling, chutneys, peanuts, and flavorful masala served inside a bun.

What makes Shree Sahjanand Dabeli special?

We focus on authentic Gujarati flavors, traditional preparation, and delicious ingredients to give you a genuine taste of Gujarat.

Do you offer different types of Dabeli?

Yes! We offer different varieties of Dabeli, including options for cheese lovers and those looking for an extra-special treat.

Is the Dabeli spicy?

Our Dabeli has a flavorful Gujarati-style spice level. You can ask us for a milder or spicier preparation.

Do you offer Jain options?

Yes, Jain options can be available. Please confirm with us while ordering.

Do you serve other items besides Dabeli?

Yes, we also serve popular Indian street-food items such as Bhel, Dahi Puri, Sev Puri, and more.

Can I place an order for a large group or event?

Yes! For bulk or event orders, please contact us in advance so we can prepare your order accordingly.

How can I contact Shree Sahjanand Dabeli?
